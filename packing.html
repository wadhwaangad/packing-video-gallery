Packing a suitcase for a trip, through contact.

One continuous simulated run. A person walks through a two-room apartment, collects eight things from the dresser, the bed, the bathroom, the kitchen and the living room, packs them into a hard-shell suitcase, swings the lid shut, zips it and stands it on its wheels. Every item is lifted and carried by finger and palm contact only.

Chapters

What the simulation does
  • MuJoCo at 1 kHz with gravity. Two anatomical MyoHand hands (23 joints each), with torque-limited finger servos and force-limited wrist carriages.
  • The model has no welds or equality constraints. Items rise, travel and settle only through contact: two-hand side squeezes for clothes, the bag, the notebook and the camera; a one-hand power grip (thumb opposing the fingers) for the bottle; finger hooks for the lid and the top handle. The lid is swung past vertical and falls shut under gravity.
  • Targets come from the live simulation state (what the person can see), not from a scripted replay of object poses.
  • The body is solved around the simulated forearms with fixed segment lengths: upper arms reach the simulated elbows exactly, legs step with look-ahead footholds, and the head camera follows the task.

Limitations
  • The body is kinematic: no balance, foot forces or whole-body collision. Pelvis clearance from walls and furniture is checked geometrically.
  • The zipper is idealised. Once the lid has fallen shut (within 2.5°) and the hand has traced the track along the near side, the lid hinge is locked closed. There is no zipper-teeth model, and the far side is not traced.
  • Garments are compliant rigid bodies (soft contact), not cloth. Masses, friction and finger strengths are assumed adult values.
  • Muscles are replaced by joint servos. Hand skin is a visual surface bound to the rigid finger bodies.
  • The footage is a path-traced render, not photoreal human capture.
All footage is simulated. Hands: MyoHub/myo_sim (Apache-2.0), adapted. Furniture, props, textures and HDRI: Poly Haven (CC0), listed in asset-manifest.json. Suitcase, garments, bag, bottle and apartment shell are procedural. Source: packing/.
