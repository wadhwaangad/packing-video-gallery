The main dataset

WEB-1K provides multiple bag-related tasks and a direct trip-suitcase task.

WorldEngine Bimanual Dataset

858 streams in the suitcase task

The task card reports 286 episodes and 858 videos for pack-suitcase-for-trip. The dataset card describes three synchronized camera streams per episode: overhead plus left- and right-wrist cameras. The wrist views provide egocentric viewpoints on bimanual manipulation.

Task matchPack items into a suitcase, zip it, and position it for carrying.

How to read this collection

Viewpoint

Robot wrist POV and human POV

The WEB-1K previews come from a robot manipulation dataset. Its full recordings have wrist-mounted cameras as well as an overhead view; this is robot-side egocentric data. The EGXO sample is human first-person footage, but covers garment fulfillment rather than luggage.

Reuse

Previews stay with their publishers

Videos are embedded from their original hosts; this gallery does not copy or rehost them. WEB-1K's data card requires access approval and lists CC BY-NC 4.0. EGXO states its public sample does not itself grant model-training rights; check each source's current terms before reuse.
