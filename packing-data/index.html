OpenPack · human · manual logistics packaging

OpenPack Dataset (+RGB)

53 hours, 16 participants, RGB plus wearable/ambient sensor streams, operation annotations, item/order and error metadata. RGB footage is academic-research-only and may not be redistributed; the non-RGB data have separate CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 terms. Treat this as a dataset lead, not an individually verified clip count.

Collection notes

  • This expanded index includes different human packing contexts: travel suitcases, warehouse cargo, and logistics packaging. Use the task filter to keep research subsets distinct.
  • Each 6D-ViCuT tile is one dataset session (28 total), not a standalone published video link. OpenPack operation annotations are not counted as separate videos.
  • 6D-ViCuT is hosted by Dryad under CC0 data terms. OpenPack RGB is academic-only and cannot be redistributed; its non-RGB files have separate CC BY-NC-SA terms.
  • Camera viewpoint is recorded where known. These stock examples are not first-person egocentric data, so they should not be mixed into an egocentric benchmark without an explicit scope change.
  • Each gallery tile links to one distinct source video. Duplicate versions and alternate encodes are not counted as additional videos.
  • Clips stay at the publisher. Pexels describes its license and permitted uses on the Pexels License page; review current terms before research use or redistribution.
  • The 500-record cap is a collection limit, not a claim that these sources form one validated academic benchmark.
Research checked 9 October 2026. Counts and access terms can change; source publishers remain authoritative. This index does not rehost videos or claim rights to source material.
