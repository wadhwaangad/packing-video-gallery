# Garment models

These four GLB models are from 3DAssets.dev's [Clothing Rail and Wardrobe](https://3dassets.dev/packs/clothing-rail-and-wardrobe) pack, published under CC0 1.0 Universal. They are bundled locally and used by the synchronized packing gallery.

- `folded-shirt.glb` — folded shirt.
- `folded-jeans.glb` — folded jeans.
- `folded-jumper.glb` — folded sweater.
- `rolled-socks.glb` — rolled pair of socks.

The gallery includes procedural fallback meshes if a local model cannot be read.
