Controller notes

Designed for a coherent replay

Named stagesMake the action readable: approach, reach, grip, lift, carry, align, lower, release, settle.

Assisted manipulationHand poses and object transport follow authored paths. This keeps the sequence repeatable without claiming a full human controller.

Release to physicsGravity, friction, contact, and limited yaw rotation act on released items. Seeking reconstructs the solver state at the selected time.

Two camera viewsThe overview explains the room route; the operator view stays with the hands. Both use one timeline and chapter list.

All imagery is generated by this deterministic simulation. It demonstrates a planned household task; it is not captured human footage. Grasped items follow authored hand paths; released items use gravity and an AABB contact solver with friction, low restitution, and yaw-only rotation. The person uses a simplified inverted-pendulum balance controller, not full-body biomechanics. Fabric deformation, fluid motion, and real suitcase capacity are not simulated.
